# AutoTeams v13.0c — Team DNA Dashboard & Atlas Health

## Updated route

- `/team-dna`

## Team DNA Dashboard

- Context-profile selector
- Confidence ring
- Profile completion
- Interview status
- Freshness and refresh status
- Context-specific trait scores
- Strength signals
- Development themes
- Atlas Health panel
- Links to complete or refresh Atlas interviews

## Atlas Health

Atlas Health evaluates:

- Core and context interview completion
- Team DNA confidence
- Profile freshness
- Matching consent

It then shows whether the profile is:

- Ready
- Beginning to age
- Due for refresh
- Incomplete

## Context comparison

Users can select two contextual profiles and compare their trait patterns.

The comparison explains that behavioural differences across contexts are expected
and do not indicate inconsistency.

## Important

The current scores are deterministic prototype indicators derived from locally
stored interview completion and answer content. Production scoring should be
implemented and validated as part of the future Atlas intelligence service.
